One in 20,000 neck adjustments leads to a stroke.

Where does that number come from? This map follows every publication that repeats it back through the sources it cites.

Reading the map
  • Gives a reference for the figure
  • Gives no reference
  • Cited as the source, but never states the figure
  • Original research paper (only Haynes has data for this figure)
  • Leads back to original data
  • Leads to a phantom source
  • Leads to a dead end with no source
  • Doubtful link: misquoted, or the source is only probable
  • Bigger dot: cited more often (area follows the count)
  • * beside a name: the paper misquotes its source (its card gives the figure it states)

Arrows point from a paper to the source it cites. Papers that cite the same source sit together, to its right.
